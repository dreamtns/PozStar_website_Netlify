PozStar Q1

PozStar Q1 (RTK GNSS with DR Function): Stable positioning even in GNSS shadow areas such as tunnels or urban environments (typically within 10 cm accuracy).

Applications: For autonomous vehicles, Road Survey, vehicle-mounted GPR (Ground Penetrating Radar), etc.
