FTA
A Free Trade Agreement (FTA) is an agreement that grants exclusive trade preferences to each other by eliminating tariffs and trade barriers on goods or services trade between the countries that have signed the agreement. If you import PozStar, Korea has 21 FTA contracts with 59 countries across the world, so you can enjoy a great benefit of tariff exemption when importing.
