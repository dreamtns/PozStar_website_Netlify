PozStar Q2-200

GNSS Full Frequency (RTK, NTRIP, PPP) + Dual Antenna (for heading support)

Applications:

Solutions requiring heading information (e.g., smart construction equipment, multibeam sonar, etc.)

Multibeam Boats

The integration of GNSS full-frequency solutions and dual-antenna heading technology significantly enhances the performance of multibeam boats, ensuring high-accuracy bathymetry and efficient operations in modern hydrographic and marine engineering projects.

Automated Construction Management System

Various types of heavy construction equipment (such as bulldozers and excavators) can utilize GNSS positioning combined with heading feedback to achieve precise path control. This enables optimized earthwork excavation and grading operations, improving efficiency and reducing resource consumption.
